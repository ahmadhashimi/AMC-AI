# Dataset and consent policy

- Keep source URL, publisher, owner, publication date, language, rights/license, permission evidence, and extraction method for *each document*.
- No blanket assumption that 'available online' means permitted for model training or open redistribution.
- School textbooks, journalism, books, and student records are not automatically public-domain.
- Separate authentic Afghan Dari from Iranian Persian as part of data quality evaluation; do not automatically discard Persian as it may help multilingual capability.
- Scan for personal data, duplicates, malware/HTML junk, corrupt OCR, and accidental test-set overlap.
- Use volunteer recordings only with informed, revocable-in-context consent consistent with applicable requirements; audio is a later modality, not included in the initial text model.
- Open-source code, open model weights, and open datasets have independent license requirements.
- Record text quality and coverage across grade levels and regions; avoid overrepresenting a single dialect or ideology.
- Keep held-out evaluation sets private from training; publish aggregate evaluation scores and methodology.
