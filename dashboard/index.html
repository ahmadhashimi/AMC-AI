How to stop and resume safely

In the training terminal, press Ctrl+C once and wait for “Stopped safely”. Keep the same settings and resume with:

.venv/bin/python -u scripts/train_epochs.py --epochs 1 --resume

This dashboard only reads measurements. Closing it does not stop your training. Checkpoints save every 1,000 steps by default.
